Conversation Stages — How to Talk at Every Stage

The full conversation, stage by stage: what stage the customer is in, what they may say, the exact reply for that stage, what happens next, and what must never be said. The reply always follows the stage — never the other way round. Quoted reply lines are the owner's own lines, in the customer's language; everything else on this page is the guide around them.

Golden Rules — True at Every Stage

ALL PAGES • ALL PATHS
1Stage first, reply secondBefore any reply, check: does an order exist? Are the documents complete? Was a payment number given? Has the owner checked the balance? The answer picks the stage — and the stage picks the reply.
2Answer only what was askedAnswer the customer's exact point first. Never dump price + rules + documents + payment all at once — reveal step by step, one easy question at a time, and keep the conversation going.
3The wait line has one home“Balance check kore janacchi” is said only ONCE, and only after the customer actually reports a payment (last 4 digit / TrxID / screenshot). A question is never answered with the wait line, and the wait line is never repeated.
4Documents gate the bKash numberThe bKash number goes out only after ALL order documents are submitted — never earlier, even if the customer asks for it directly.
5Paid = owner-confirmed onlyA payment is never confirmed by the chat alone. The owner checks his own bKash balance and presses Payment Received — only then is the order confirmed and marked Paid.
6Voice of every replySoft and friendly, always — every “no” included. Address the customer as “sir”, use their name only at attention moments, no emoji, and reply in ONE language only: full Bangla, full English, or Banglish — never mixed, never another language.

Before Any Payment Reply — 4 Checks

PAYMENT STAGES ONLY
  • 1
    Order created? No → the payment cannot be checked at all. Use Stage 0 — the owner's direct question. No wait line, no balance check.
  • 2
    Payment number given by us? No → same Stage 0. Money we never asked for, to a number we never gave, has nothing to match against.
  • 3
    Is the customer asking a question, or reporting a payment? A question (for example “Last number ki abar pathabo”) gets its answer first — Stage 10. Only a real payment report moves to the wait stage.
  • 4
    Has the wait line already been sent for this report? Yes → never send it again. Answer the new message on its own point and wait for the owner's Received / Not Received.

Label After Every Stage Change

ONE LABEL PER CHAT
New customer→ Interested→ Documents Pending→ Documents Complete→ Pending payment→ Paid (owner-confirmed only)→ Order complete→ Delivered

Keep exactly one current-stage label on each chat and update it at every stage change. A warm close or a 24-hour retry carries the Follow-up label instead. The stages above follow this same pipeline — the label and the way of talking always move together.
